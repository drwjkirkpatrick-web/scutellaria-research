**Prepared:** 2026-11-14. All sources were fetched, page text saved under `research/epigenetics/pages/` before use, and every quote in `research/findings_epigenetics.json` (58 sources, 30 claims) extracted programmatically from the saved files and re-verified as an exact substring. ## 1. Overview

The major *S. baicalensis* flavones — baicalein, baicalin, wogonin, oroxylin A, and the minor skullcapflavone I — act on every major layer of the epigenetic machinery described in the modern literature:

- **DNA methylation:** a convergent DNMT1 story — promoter demethylation with gene re-expression is documented for four promoters (SHP-1, GABRD, SCARA5, KEAP1) plus PPARγ derepression through MeCP2/EZH2.
- **Histone chemistry:** baicalein inhibits HDAC-1/8; wogonin degrades HDAC1/2 and down-regulates the acetyltransferase p300 (with loss of histone H3/H4 crotonylation); baicalein suppresses H3K4me3 through adenosylhomocysteinase (AHCY) inhibition and stabilizes the H3K9me3 demethylase KDM4E; baicalin is a characterized LSD1 (KDM1A) inhibitor, and wogonin blocks LSD1-mediated NF-κB p65 demethylation.
- **Non-coding RNA:** miR-21 suppression (baicalein), miR-139-3p induction (baicalein and baicalin), and broad miRNA-profile remodeling, plus lncRNA (MALAT1, SNHG1, AK021954) and circRNA (CirSLC7A6, circ_0000734) effects.
- **Whole-transcriptome:** microarray, RNA-seq, lncRNA-seq, and single-cell signatures; an Nrf2/ARE hepatic program (2012) and a JAK-STAT program in aging SAMP8 mice (2019).
- **Epitranscriptomics:** baicalein down-regulates the m6A demethylase FTO.

The classical anchor is the 2001 demonstration that baicalein, baicalin, and wogonin inhibit inducible iNOS gene expression in LPS-stimulated RAW 264.7 macrophages, with only wogonin also blocking COX-2 gene expression — the field's founding gene-expression finding for this herb (Wogonin, baicalin, and baicalein inhibition of inducible nitric oxide synthase… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11331078/, accessed 2026-11-14).

**Evidence-quality map (critical):** this literature is oncology-skewed — most load-bearing findings come from cancer cell lines. Non-cancer in vivo models exist (status-epilepticus, pulmonary-fibrosis, arthritis, osteoarthritis, and diabetic-nephropathy rodents; SAMP8 aging mice; *C. elegans*), but no interventional human study measures epigenetic endpoints, and none measures epigenetic age or DNA-methylation clocks after *Scutellaria* exposure (see §4).

## 2. Evidence in depth

### 2.1 DNA methylation — a convergent DNMT1 story

Four independent promoter-demethylation accounts converge on DNMT1 rather than the de novo methyltransferases. In imatinib-resistant chronic myeloid leukemia CD34+ cells, baicalein induced DNMT1-mediated demethylation of the SHP-1 promoter with re-expression of SHP-1 and inhibition of JAK2/STAT5, with molecular docking supporting direct DNMT1 binding (Induction of DNMT1-dependent demethylation of SHP-1… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36869331/, accessed 2026-11-14). In a rat lithium-pilocarpine model of status epilepticus — a non-cancer, neurological model — baicalein interacted with DNMT1 to suppress methylation of the GABRD promoter, restoring GABRD protein in hippocampus (Baicalein Alleviates Lithium-Pilocarpine-Induced Status Epilepticus… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40569104/, accessed 2026-11-14). In hepatic stellate cells and mouse fibrosis models, baicalein targeted DNMT1-facilitated methylation of the CpG-island-bearing SCARA5 promoter, promoting SCARA5 expression and ferroptosis of activated stellate cells (Baicalein facilitates hepatic stellate cell ferroptosis… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41270385/, accessed 2026-11-14). For wogonin, the demethylation target sits in the antioxidant pathway: wogonin reduced CpG methylation in the KEAP1 promoter, raising Keap1 protein and suppressing Nrf2/HIF-1α-driven radioresistance in breast cancer models (Wogonin Diminishes Radioresistance of Breast Cancer… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37903716/, accessed 2026-11-14). Separately, baicalin (in combination with rosmarinic acid, from the formula Yang-Gan-Wan) epigenetically derepressed PPARγ in hepatic stellate cells by reducing MeCP2 expression and its recruitment to the Pparγ promoter and suppressing the PRC2 methyltransferase EZH2, with reduced H3K27 dimethylation — the only EZH2/H3K27 signal in this literature, and notably a two-compound combination, not a single constituent (Rosmarinic acid and baicalin epigenetically derepress PPARγ… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22095555/, accessed 2026-11-14).

### 2.2 Histone acetylation and deacetylation

Baicalein is a characterized **HDAC-1/8 inhibitor**: in core-binding-factor AML it inhibited HDAC-1/8, with mild direct HDAC-1 inhibition plus ubiquitin-proteasome HDAC1 degradation, up-regulating histone H3 acetylation without inducing ABC transporter genes (Natural HDAC-1/8 inhibitor baicalein… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32898337/, accessed 2026-11-14); the same group extended this to cutaneous T-cell lymphoma cells, restoring p53 acetylation through HDAC8 inhibition (Involvement of p53 Acetylation in Growth Suppression… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32119867/, accessed 2026-11-14). Wogonin instead decreased HDAC1 and HDAC2 protein levels in A549 lung adenocarcinoma cells (Wogonin has multiple anti-cancer effects… HDAC1/HDAC2… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24265759/, accessed 2026-11-14) and, in the most distinctive finding, suppressed latent HIV-1 reactivation in latency models and primary CD4+ T cells from ART-suppressed individuals ex vivo by inhibiting expression of the histone acetyltransferase p300 and decreasing histone H3/H4 **crotonylation** at the viral promoter (Wogonin inhibits latent HIV-1 reactivation by downregulating histone crotonylation — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37172478/, accessed 2026-11-14). Deacetylation crosstalk with NF-κB is documented for baicalin: in olfactory bulbectomized rats and LPS-stimulated BV-2 microglia it decreased p65 acetylation, including at lysine 310, via SIRT1 (Baicalin reverse depressive-like behaviors… SIRT1-NF-kB… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30848526/, accessed 2026-11-14), and in collagen-induced arthritis rats and human rheumatoid synoviocytes p65 acetylation fell in tandem with sirt1 up-regulation (Inhibitory effect of baicalin on collagen-induced arthritis… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24893986/, accessed 2026-11-14). Longevity-relevant: baicalein was one of six glucose-toxicity-protective drugs that extended *C. elegans* lifespan, and those protective effects were blocked by RNAi inhibition of cbp-1, the worm ortholog of the CBP/p300 acetyltransferase (FDA-approved drugs that protect mammalian neurons… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22114686/, accessed 2026-11-14) — the only direct lifespan endpoint in this stream, and it is invertebrate.

### 2.3 Histone methylation and demethylases

Baicalein suppresses **H3K4me3** indirectly through methyl metabolism: it directly inhibits AHCY (adenosylhomocysteinase) at residues D134/D303, causing S-adenosylhomocysteine accumulation and decreased H3K4me3 with down-regulation of OCT4/Nanog stemness in microsatellite-instability colorectal cancer, validated in patient tissues and PDX models (Baicalein specifically suppresses microsatellite instability colorectal cancer… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41038143/, accessed 2026-11-14). In the opposite direction, baicalein enhanced the demethylase KDM4E in triple-negative breast cancer cells, reducing H3K9me3 at the BICD1 promoter (Baicalein promotes KDM4E to induce BICD1… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38607237/, accessed 2026-11-14). Baicalin was first characterized as a natural **LSD1 inhibitor** (IC50 3.01 μM, strong cellular inhibition) (Baicalin, a natural LSD1 inhibitor — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27814566/, accessed 2026-11-14), and wogonin bound LSD1/KDM1A in osteoarthritis models, disrupting LSD1-mediated NF-κB p65 demethylation and thereby inhibiting JNK/NF-κB activation (Wogonin modulates macrophage polarization… through the LSD1-p65 axis… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40818185/, accessed 2026-11-14). Honest counterweight: in a 2026 study baicalin acted as an independent fibrogenic factor and *aggravated* isoproterenol-induced myocardial fibrosis via LSD1/AP-1 signaling with increased histone H3 Ser10 phosphorylation — epigenetic modulation by these flavones is not uniformly beneficial and is context- and dose-dependent (Baicalin aggravates isoproterenol-induced myocardial fibrosis… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501551/, accessed 2026-11-14).

### 2.4 microRNAs

**Baicalein** suppresses **miR-21** in TGF-β1-treated human lung fibroblasts (via the miR-21 transcription factor STAT3), with miR-21 mimics blunting its anti-fibrotic effect (Baicalein represses TGF-β1-induced fibroblast differentiation… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30201452/, accessed 2026-11-14) and, in vivo, in bleomycin pulmonary fibrosis rats (Baicalein attenuates bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis… inhibition of miR-21 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23523661/, accessed 2026-11-14). High-throughput sequencing in Panc-1 cells showed miR-**139-3p** (not the hinted 5p arm) as the most up-regulated and miR-196b-5p as the most down-regulated miRNA, acting via NOB1 and ING5 (Baicalein Induces Apoptosis of Pancreatic Cancer Cells… miR-139-3p and miR-196b-5p — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33996574/, accessed 2026-11-14). Baicalein also broadly alters miRNA profiles in Bel-7402 hepatoma cells, with targets enriched in MAPK, PI3K-Akt, Wnt, Hippo and mTOR pathways (Baicalein… Alters MicroRNA Expression Profiles in Bel-7402… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28351032/, accessed 2026-11-14), up-regulates miR-29 in fibroblasts (Baicalein retards proliferation and collagen deposition… via microRNA-29 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31081145/, accessed 2026-11-14), down-regulates miR-424-3p to raise PTEN (Baicalein inhibits cell growth… via miR-424-3p… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29392841/, accessed 2026-11-14), and up-regulates miR-3663-3p targeting SH3GL1 (MiR-3663-3p participates in the anti-hepatocellular carcinoma… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33838155/, accessed 2026-11-14). **Baicalin** up-regulates **miR-139-3p** (targeting CDK16) in colon cancer cells and xenografts (Baicalin Blocks Colon Cancer Cell Cycle… miR-139-3p Upregulation… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36599649/, accessed 2026-11-14) and miR-124 (targeting TLR4) in diabetic-nephropathy mice and HK-2 tubule cells (Baicalin suppresses renal fibrosis through microRNA-124/TLR4/NF-κB… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32500512/, accessed 2026-11-14). Direction is context-dependent: in LPS-stimulated H9c2 cardiomyoblasts baicalin further *elevated* miR-21 for an anti-inflammatory PDCD4/JNK effect (Baicalin relieves lipopolysaccharide-evoked inflammatory injury… miR-21… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31984561/, accessed 2026-11-14) — the same miRNA baicalein suppresses in fibrosis. Baicalin also reshaped miRNA profiles in UVB-irradiated mouse skin in vivo (Baicalin modulates microRNA expression in UVB irradiated mouse skin — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23554741/, accessed 2026-11-14) and in normal porcine aortic endothelial cells (The effect of baicalin on microRNA expression profiles in porcine aortic… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32519231/, accessed 2026-11-14). **Wogonin** up-regulated miR-27b-5p (70 miRNAs changed >2-fold by small-RNA-seq), which directly targeted YWHAZ in hepatoma cells (Wogonin inhibits hepatoma cell proliferation… miR-27b-5p/YWHAZ… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37623816/, accessed 2026-11-14) and down-regulated miR-155 in EBV+ lymphoma (Wogonin as a targeted therapeutic agent for EBV (+) lymphoma… miR-155/PU.1… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28222771/, accessed 2026-11-14). **Oroxylin A** regulated miR-155-5p (targeting IRF2BP2) in LPS lung injury (Inhibition of miR-155 potentially protects… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33052070/, accessed 2026-11-14) and, as a CDK9 inhibitor, suppressed p53-driven biogenesis of the miR-183/96/182 cluster after stroke (Suppression of neuronal CDK9/p53/VDAC signaling… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39235466/, accessed 2026-11-14). **Skullcapflavone I** down-regulated miR-23a in Panc-1 cells (Skullcapflavone I has a potent anti-pancreatic cancer activity… miR-23a — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32141657/, accessed 2026-11-14). A PRISMA systematic review (15 studies) confirms baicalein's miRNA modulation across hepatocellular, breast, cervical, ovarian and gastric cancers (The effects of flavonoid baicalein on miRNA expressions in cancer: a systematic review — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40153015/, accessed 2026-11-14), alongside broader reviews (Regulation of Cell Signaling Pathways and Non-Coding RNAs by Baicalein… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35955525/, accessed 2026-11-14; The most recent progress of baicalein… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38850656/, accessed 2026-11-14).

### 2.5 lncRNAs and circRNAs

Baicalin down-regulated lncRNA-MALAT1 with miR-200c up-regulation in breast cancer cells (Baicalin suppresses the migration and invasion… lncRNA-MALAT1/miR-200c… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36401368/, accessed 2026-11-14); baicalein acted through the circular RNA CirSLC7A6/miR-2682-5p/SLC7A6 axis in cisplatin-resistant ovarian cancer cells and xenografts (Baicalein improves the chemoresistance of ovarian cancer… CirSLC7A6 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37940982/, accessed 2026-11-14) and increased lncRNA AK021954 in vascular smooth muscle cells (a non-cancer, atherosclerosis-relevant model) (Baicalein inhibits VSMCs proliferation via regulating LncRNA AK021954… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26885187/, accessed 2026-11-14). Tumor lncRNA context determines drug response: lncRNA SNHG1 attenuated baicalein's anti-cancer effect via miR-3127-5p/FZD4/Wnt in cervical cancer (SNHG1 represses the anti-cancer roles of baicalein… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32883110/, accessed 2026-11-14). In human rheumatoid material, baicalin targeted circ_0000734/miR-197-5p/IKBKB in RA fibroblast-like synoviocytes, with circ_0000734 levels in patient PBMCs correlating with RF, anti-CCP, CRP, ESR and DAS28 (Baicalin improves the inflammatory response of RA-FLS… circ_0000734… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42292404/, accessed 2026-11-14). In-silico ceRNA network reanalysis identified 14 differentially expressed lncRNAs and 26 miRNAs after baicalein in HCC (Functional lncRNA-miRNA-mRNA Networks in Response to Baicalein… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33511213/, accessed 2026-11-14), and in normal piglet aortic endothelial cells baicalin pretreatment shifted 107 lncRNAs and 936 genes under bacterial/LPS challenge (Effect of Baicalin on Transcriptome Changes in Piglet Vascular Endothelial Cells… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34029124/, accessed 2026-11-14).

### 2.6 Whole-transcriptome studies

An Affymetrix 42K microarray in HepG2 cells showed baicalein up-regulating 440 and down-regulating 254 gene probes, with Nrf2/ARE the top hit and ARE-luciferase transactivation confirmed (Gene expression profiling and pathway network analysis of hepatic metabolic enzymes… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22265932/, accessed 2026-11-14). In senescence-accelerated SAMP8 mice — the most directly aging-relevant transcriptome study — baicalein regulated fifty hub genes with JAK-STAT most significant, and prevented JAK2/STAT1 activation while lowering Aβ1-42 and RAGE in cortex (Integration of transcriptomics and network analysis… SAMP8 — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31715136/, accessed 2026-11-14). Baicalin RNA-seq in gastric cancer cells shifted 68 lncRNAs, 20 miRNAs and 1648 mRNAs, dominated by the p53 pathway (Systematic Understanding of the Mechanism of Baicalin… Transcriptome Analysis — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34337018/, accessed 2026-11-14), and a single-cell/spatial-transcriptomics analysis defined a baicalein-responsive 10-gene NSCLC signature (Integrative single-cell and spatial transcriptomics analysis… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42251782/, accessed 2026-11-14). At the whole-extract level, *S. baicalensis* extract (with scutellarin and oroxylin A, baicalein the main TAS2R4 agonist) transcriptionally regulated bitter-taste receptors in asthmatic rats — extract-level transcriptional regulation, not a purified epigenetic study (Scutellaria baicalensis ameliorates allergic airway inflammation… TAS2Rs — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39362328/, accessed 2026-11-14).

### 2.7 Epitranscriptomics (m6A)

Baicalein down-regulated the m6A demethylase FTO, enhancing m6A modification of ZEB1 mRNA and reducing its stability, disrupting TGF-β-induced EMT in pancreatic cancer — the first RNA-methylation (rather than DNA-methylation) layer for this herb (Baicalein disrupts TGF-β-induced EMT… FTO-dependent m6A… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40262723/, accessed 2026-11-14).

### 2.8 Nrf2/KEAP1 epigenetic crosstalk

Baicalein is an Nrf2 activator in Hct116 cells, but the 2016 mechanism study found **no** change in NRF2 gene transcription, ubiquitinylation or proteasomal activity — instead baicalein decreased Nrf2 phosphorylation (IC50 ≈ 20 μM), consistent with kinase inhibition (Modulation of the Nrf2 signalling pathway… baicalein and its methylated derivatives — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27143122/, accessed 2026-11-14). The one clear demethylation event in this pathway is wogonin's KEAP1-promoter hypomethylation (§2.1). In non-cancer cardiomyocyte models baicalein activated Nrf2/SLC7A11/GPX4 to inhibit ferroptosis (Baicalein Attenuates High Glucose and Sodium Palmitate-Induced Ferroptosis… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42511738/, accessed 2026-11-14), whereas in cisplatin-treated NSCLC baicalin induced KEAP1 accumulation and NRF2/HO-1 reduction to aid chemo-sensitization — the opposite Nrf2 direction in an oncology context (Baicalin promotes the sensitivity of NSCLC to cisplatin… KEAP1-NRF2/HO-1… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39061086/, accessed 2026-11-14).

## 3. Human evidence

No interventional human study of any *Scutellaria* constituent with an epigenetic readout exists. The closest human-material data: (i) wogonin suppressed latent HIV-1 reactivation in primary CD4+ T cells from ART-suppressed individuals ex vivo via p300/crotonylation (Wogonin inhibits latent HIV-1 reactivation… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37172478/, accessed 2026-11-14); (ii) in patient-derived colorectal liver metastasis specimens, pharmacological LSD1 inhibition with baicalin reduced CD206 and PD-L1 in tumor-associated macrophages (Lysine-specific demethylase 1 (LSD1) regulates macrophage plasticity… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42900293/, accessed 2026-11-14); (iii) human RA synoviocytes and patient PBMC correlations (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24893986/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42292404/, accessed 2026-11-14); (iv) clinical MSI-CRC tissues validating the AHCY/H3K4me3 axis (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41038143/, accessed 2026-11-14); and (v) primary CML/AML cells (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36869331/, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32898337/, accessed 2026-11-14). All are ex vivo or in vitro with human material. The *C. elegans* lifespan finding (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22114686/, accessed 2026-11-14) is the only direct longevity endpoint and is invertebrate.

## 4. What is NOT established (gaps and failed hints)

- **Epigenetic age / DNA methylation clocks:** NOT FOUND. Targeted PubMed searches (Scutellaria/baicalein/baicalin/wogonin/oroxylin × "epigenetic clock", "methylation clock", "DNA methylation age", "biological age clock", "epigenetic age", Horvath) returned zero constituent-specific studies. This is a documented gap, not a silence — no study has tested whether *Scutellaria* exposure shifts any methylation clock.
- **Baicalein → NIS/SLC5A5 promoter demethylation in thyroid cancer:** NOT FOUND. The only PubMed hit for baicalein+NIS+thyroid is a quercetin study in BCPAP cells (no demethylation mechanism) (The flavonoid quercetin reduces cell migration and increases NIS… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33831503/, accessed 2026-11-14).
- **Baicalein re-expression of p16INK4a or RASSF1A via promoter demethylation:** NOT FOUND as an epigenetic mechanism. Baicalein up-regulates p16 protein in HUVECs and p15(Ink4B) in rat heart endothelial cells without any methylation readout (Baicalein potently suppresses angiogenesis… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21659383/, accessed 2026-11-14; Baicalein induces a dual growth arrest… — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11513834/, accessed 2026-11-14); no RASSF1A study exists.
- **DNMT3a/DNMT3b modulation:** NOT FOUND — all promoter-demethylation evidence converges on DNMT1.
- **HDAC3 inhibition:** NOT FOUND — the reported isoforms are HDAC1/8 for baicalein and HDAC1/2 for wogonin.
- **Direct baicalein inhibition of p300/CBP/PCAF (HAT inhibition):** NOT FOUND. Wogonin (not baicalein) down-regulates p300; baicalein's worm lifespan benefit instead *requires* cbp-1 (CBP/p300 ortholog).
- **EZH2/H3K27me3 for single constituents:** NOT FOUND — EZH2 suppression with reduced H3K27 dimethylation is documented only for baicalin combined with rosmarinic acid (Yang-Gan-Wan), not for a single Scutellaria flavone (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22095555/, accessed 2026-11-14).
- **NRF2 promoter demethylation:** NOT FOUND — the demethylated promoter in this pathway is KEAP1 (wogonin) (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37903716/, accessed 2026-11-14).
- **Concentrations and translatability:** most in vitro epigenetic readouts use 25–100 μM, far above reported plasma levels after oral dosing; no PK-bridged epigenetic study exists. Context risk is real: baicalin aggravated isoproterenol-induced myocardial fibrosis via LSD1/AP-1 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42501551/, accessed 2026-11-14), and miR-21 moves in opposite directions depending on constituent and tissue (§2.4).

## 6. Open questions for the parent

1. **DNMT1 convergence:** the four promoter-demethylation stories all converge on DNMT1 — recommend framing DNMT1 (not DNMTs generally) as the methylation target, with the p300/wogonin and LSD1/baicalin axes as the acetylation and demethylation complements.
2. **Longevity framing:** the C. elegans lifespan/CBP-1 finding is the only direct longevity endpoint — it belongs in the foundation section but the longevity stream (brief-03) may also claim it; coordinate to avoid duplication.
3. **Direction-of-effect honesty:** miR-21 (baicalein down vs baicalin up) and Nrf2 (baicalein activates in non-cancer contexts vs baicalin suppresses in chemo contexts) move in opposite directions by context — the paper should present these as context-dependent, not contradictory "errors."
4. **The baicalin fibrosis aggravation (LSD1/AP-1)** is an adverse-direction epigenetic finding; recommend the safety stream (brief-06) also carries it, and the foundation section references it as an honesty anchor.
5. **m6A/FTO (Zhao 2025):** include as a short epitranscriptomics layer or fold into gene-expression regulation? It is mechanistically adjacent but currently a single paper.
6. **The extract-level TAS2R study (Qiao 2025)** is whole-plant transcriptional regulation — decide whether the epigenetics foundation should cite extract-level data or remain constituent-only.
7. **Epigenetic clocks:** the paper should state explicitly that no methylation-clock study of *Scutellaria* exists — this is the single most important gap for the longevity claim and should be a labeled limitation, not omitted.
